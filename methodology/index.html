Methodology

Discipline before conclusions.

How ORION grades evidence, handles uncertainty, and earns the right to be read.

Source grades

ConfirmedMultiple sources / verified. Supported by primary source, multiple reliable sources, or direct artifact / system record.
Single-sourceOne source / useful, but not confirmed. Reported by one source or one relayed record; useful but not independently confirmed.
NarrativeInterpretive / speculative. Symbolic interpretation, hypothesis, pattern read, cultural resonance, or unverified framing. Must not be treated as fact. Not a confirmed fact.

Fact vs. analysis vs. speculation

Facts are graded claims — what happened, with receipts. Analysis is what the facts suggest, stated plainly and separately. Speculation is what might follow, labeled narrative and never smuggled into the fact column. Watchlist items are monitored, not judged. Spiritual and symbolic reflections live in the narrative layer, never in the confirmed one.

Publication rule

No item moves from draft to published without MASTER INDY’s explicit approval. Drafts are staged, reviewed, and graded — publication is his decision alone.

Publication warning. Draft intelligence records are not approved for publication until MASTER INDY explicitly approves them.

The symbolic layer

ORION carries mythic resonance — watcher, navigator, interlinker — as identity flavor and internal orientation. It is always labeled narrative, never presented as confirmed fact. The observatory stays sober; the symbolism stays in its lane.
